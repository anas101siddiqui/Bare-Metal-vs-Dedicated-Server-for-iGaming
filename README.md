## 🔍 Bare Metal and Dedicated Server Are the Same, But the Difference Is in the Delivery
 
Here is the honest answer that you will not always get from a sales page. At the hardware level, bare metal and dedicated servers are **the same thing**. The difference lies in how the server is delivered, billed, and managed.
 
Both terms describe a physical server reserved for one customer. No other tenant shares its CPU, memory, storage, or network port. You are not renting a slice of a machine. You are renting the **whole machine**. 🖥️
 
So why do two names exist?
 
- 📜 **Dedicated server** comes from the early days of web hosting, when customers leased a physical machine from a hosting company, usually on a monthly contract.
- ☁️ **Bare metal** became popular later, in the cloud era. It describes physical servers delivered with cloud style speed and automation, running without a virtualization layer between your software and the hardware.
In practice, the difference usually shows up in five areas:
 
| ⚙️ Area | 🔩 Bare Metal (often) | 🖥️ Dedicated Server (often) |
|:---|:---|:---|
| 🚀 **Provisioning** | Automated, ready in minutes | Set up by hand, hours or days |
| 💳 **Billing** | Hourly or short term options | Monthly, better price on longer terms |
| 🎛️ **Management** | API, dashboard, automation | Control panel and support team |
| 🧩 **Customization** | Mostly standard catalog | Often custom builds |
| 🧱 **Virtualization** | Direct hardware access, no hypervisor | Same, or install your own hypervisor |
 
> [!IMPORTANT]
> 🎯 These differences depend on the **provider**, not on the label. One company's dedicated server may be delivered in minutes, while another company's bare metal server may need a long contract. Always read what is actually included, not just the name on the product page.
 
> [!WARNING]
> 👀 **Watch out for look alikes.** Some marketing uses the word "dedicated" for virtual servers with reserved resources. That is **not** a physical server. If a plan does not clearly say you get the entire physical machine, ask before you buy.

## 🖥️ What Is a Dedicated Server in iGaming?
 
A dedicated server is the classic way to run a serious gaming platform on single-tenant hardware. It is a physical machine that your hosting provider leases to you, and only you.
 
For an iGaming operator, this usually means one or more servers that run the core of the business: the game engine, the database, the wallet and payment services, the bet settlement logic, and the back office. You get full control over the operating system, the software stack, and the security settings.
 
**✅ Strengths of the classic dedicated model:**
 
- 💰 **Predictable monthly cost:** You know what you will pay each month, which makes budgeting easier for an always-on platform.
- 🧩 **Custom hardware:** Many providers let you choose the CPU, RAM, drives, and network port that match your workload.
- 🧑‍💻 **Human support:** A support team can handle hardware issues, reboots, and network questions.
- 🏛️ **Stability for long term platforms:** A core casino or sportsbook platform rarely changes every week, so a stable monthly setup suits it well.
**⚠️ Trade-off:** flexibility. If you need to double your capacity for a single weekend, a classic monthly contract may not be the most convenient way to do it.

<a id="bare-metal"></a>
 
## ☁️ What Is Bare Metal in iGaming?
 
Bare metal is a physical server delivered **without a virtualization layer** on top of it. Your software talks directly to the hardware, so nothing sits between your game engine and the CPU.
 
The term also became linked with a delivery style. Many bare metal offers let you pick a server from a catalog, deploy it through a dashboard or an API, and have it ready quickly. Some even allow hourly billing, which feels like the cloud, but with the power and isolation of a real machine.
 
**🎯 Where it helps iGaming teams:**
 
- 🚀 Launching a new brand or market quickly
- 🧪 Building a test or staging environment that looks exactly like production
- 📈 Adding short term capacity during a big tournament or promotion
- 🤖 Running automated deployments with infrastructure as code
**⚠️ Trade-offs:** Standard catalogs may limit customization. And for a platform that runs all day, every day, hourly pricing can end up costing more than a long term monthly plan.

## 🧨 Common Myths About Bare Metal and Dedicated Servers
 
Because the two terms are often mixed up, a few myths keep floating around. Let us bust the most common ones. 💥
 
<details>
<summary><b>❌ Myth 1: "Bare metal is always faster than a dedicated server."</b></summary>

## 🔐 Security and DDoS Protection: What Single-Tenant Does and Does Not Do
 
Security is a top concern for every real-money platform. It is important to understand what a physical, single-tenant server gives you and what it does not.
 
**✅ What you get:** isolation. No other customer shares your hardware, so you avoid the risks that come from other tenants on the same machine. You also control the operating system, the firewall rules, and who can log in.
 
**🚫 What you do not get automatically:** protection from every threat. A dedicated or bare metal server will not stop a large DDoS attack on its own, because the attack hits your network connection before it ever reaches your software.
 
> [!WARNING]
> 🌊 A single-tenant server isolates you from other tenants. It does **not** make you immune to DDoS attacks.
 
**🧱 A strong iGaming security setup uses several layers:**
 
- [x] 🛡️ **Network level protection:** hardware or upstream filtering that absorbs and blocks attack traffic
- [x] 🔥 **Firewall and rate limiting:** rules that limit abusive requests and block suspicious sources
- [x] 🕸️ **Web application firewall:** protection for login pages, payment forms, and APIs
- [x] 🔄 **Regular patching and monitoring:** updates and alerts that catch problems early
- [x] 💾 **Backups and a recovery plan:** a tested way to restore your platform if something goes wrong
When you compare providers, ask exactly **what DDoS protection is included** and what happens when an attack is larger than the included level. 🎯
